Due Diligence & Verification

Know who you are dealing with before committing capital. We combine documentary review, corporate checks, field verification, and commercial reputation work to give decision-makers a grounded view of the counterparty.

SERVICE MANDATE

Verification is designed for situations where a company profile, pitch deck, quotation, or remote presentation is not enough. We test the claims that matter to the transaction and distinguish documented facts from field observations.

Every engagement is scoped around a defined commercial question, decision point, or execution requirement. We can structure the work as a focused research brief, a field assignment, a transaction-support mandate, or an ongoing local operating layer.

CORE WORKSTREAMS
  • check_circleMinistry of Law (AHU) Corporate Records
  • check_circleOn-Site Physical Asset & Inventory Audits
  • check_circleReputation, Litigation, and Sanction Checks

How We Structure an Engagement

1. Define the decision and success criteria. 2. Establish the geographic, sectoral, and counterparty scope. 3. Conduct desk research and local validation. 4. Coordinate fieldwork where required. 5. Deliver findings, evidence, risks, and recommended next actions.

What You Receive

A decision-oriented output rather than a generic directory: structured findings, relevant benchmarks or verification notes, identified risks and constraints, and a clear set of practical next steps tailored to the Indonesian operating context.
